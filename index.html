Steps

  1. Add the photos. They fill the cells in the order you picked them. Swap trades two over, and Before and After stay on their own side.
  2. Pick a tool. It decides what dragging on the picture does and shows only the controls for that job.
  3. Photos: drag a photo inside its cell to move it, and scroll or pinch to zoom.
  4. Line up: in Swipe, pick which photo moves, then drag it, nudge it or set its Size. Divider slides the divider instead. In Grid, Zoom works on All photos zooms them together to the same spot.
  5. Cover: drag over the eyes, a name or an address.
  6. Mark: drag to draw an arrow, circle, line or box. Drag a mark to move it, or an end to reshape it.
  7. Video: Record the swipe runs the divider across by itself. Record while I drag follows what you do until you press Stop.
  8. More under each tool holds the settings you need less often.
  9. Press Save.
