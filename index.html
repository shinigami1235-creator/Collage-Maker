Steps

  1. Add the photos. They fill the cells in the order you picked them.
  2. Drag inside a cell to move the photo, and scroll or pinch to zoom.
  3. Zoom set to All photos moves them together, which lines up the same spot on a before and after.
  4. In Swipe, set Dragging the picture to a photo to line the two up, and use the nudge buttons.
  5. Dragging the picture set to the divider slides the divider. The button under it puts the divider back in the middle.
  6. In Swipe, Dragging the picture also decides which photo the Size slider, the scroll wheel and a pinch act on.
  7. Swap trades the two photos over. Before and After stay on their own side.
  8. Press Save.
