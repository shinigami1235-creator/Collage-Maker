Steps

  1. Add the photos, or paste one with Ctrl+V. They fill the cells in the order you picked them. Swap trades two over, and Before and After stay on their own side. New set clears the photos with their covers, marks and labels, and needs a second press.
  2. Pick a tool. It decides what dragging on the picture does and shows only the controls for that job.
  3. Photos: drag a photo inside its cell to move it, and scroll or pinch to zoom.
  4. Background: Remove the background finds the person on every photo, or anything that stands apart from a plain background. Draw around it takes a loose loop drawn a little outside the subject and moves it in onto the subject's edge, for a hand, a product or a busy background. The line can keep what is inside, add it back or take it away, and drawing stays on until Done. The Erase and Restore brushes fix small bits by hand, and Undo and Redo step through every change. The removed part shows the background colour, and shows in faint red on screen while the Photos tool is open.
  5. Line up: in Swipe, drag a photo to move it or drag the divider to slide it. The photo you touch becomes the one picked under Moves, and the arrows, Size and Turn work on it. Both moves the two together. In Grid, Zoom works on All photos zooms them together to the same spot.
  6. Auto line up matches small spots across the photos and moves, sizes and turns each photo onto the first, even one taken on its side. It runs by itself when the second photo of a pair goes in. Auto line up works on the main subject, the person or the arm, so a floor or a wall that moved does not pull it off. Line up on a part has you draw around something on the first photo, like a scar or a mole, and finds the same part on the other photos even when they are turned or closer. Two-point line up has you tap the same two spots on each photo instead, like both pupils. Four-point line up takes four spots and also matches the camera angle, for a photo taken from the side. A magnifier shows under your finger while you tap. When the first photo has its background removed, the photos lined up on it get the same cut.
  7. View: See-through lays the second photo over the first at half strength and Differences shows only where they differ. Both are for checking the line up and never show in the saved picture.
  8. Fill the frame zooms the photos together until no background shows. Centre both puts the photos back in the middle at their own size. Reset and line up does the same and then runs Auto line up again. Undo or Ctrl+Z takes back the last move, size or turn.
  9. Labels: a label and a date for each photo. The date comes from the photo when it holds one, and you can change it. Credit puts a line like "Cygnus Aesthetics" along the top or bottom of the picture, and Watermark lays words, a symbol or your logo over every photo. Only the labels differ from photo to photo. With Labels open, tap a label, the credit or the watermark to pick it, drag it to put it anywhere, and use its round handle to tilt it and its square corner to size it.
  10. Cover: Cover the eyes finds the eyes on every photo and covers them with the cover picked, a bar, a blur, an oval or a block. Each eye gets its own cover and the bridge of the nose stays clear, unless Eyes is set to Both eyes together. Press it after lining up, since a cover stays where it was put when a photo moves. Or drag over the eyes, a name or an address. A cover lands on the same spot of every photo, or only on its own with This photo only. Drag a cover to move it. The picked cover has a round handle to tilt it and a square corner to size it.
  11. Mark: drag to draw an arrow, circle, line or box. With Shows on set to Both photos it appears on every photo at the same spot. Drag a mark to move it, or an end to reshape it.
  12. Video: Record the swipe runs the divider across by itself. Record while I drag follows what you do until you press Stop.
  13. Full screen: one photo close up, with arrows that move a pixel per tap and buttons that turn it a tenth of a degree.
  14. More under each tool holds the settings you need less often. The app keeps your settings for next time, while the photos, labels and dates are not kept.
  15. Press Save, or Share on a phone to send the picture straight to another app.
